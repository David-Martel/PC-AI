### F7: WdfDirectedPowerTransitionEnable=1 on I2C 7E78 controller — residual after T1
Evidence: hid-deep-09:80
- REG: HKLM:\...7E78\Device Parameters\WDF\WdfDirectedPowerTransitionEnable=1
- This setting allows the Runtime Power Framework (PoFx) / Platform Energy Policy to direct the WDF driver (iaLPSS2_I2C_MTL) into D3cold independently of the MSPower_DeviceEnable flag
- Modern Standby with Connected Standby active: PEP can still clock-gate/power-gate the I2C controller even with power-down disabled at the device level
- Net effect: T1 reduced but did NOT eliminate the power-gating pathway for the touchpad controller
